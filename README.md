# FreedomOS — The Autonomous Financial Freedom Operating System

> *"A system to systematically eliminate debt, supercharge savings velocity, and achieve mathematical Financial Independence (FIRE)."*

---

## ⚡ What is FreedomOS?

FreedomOS is a private, local-first web application and personal CFO engineered to help you achieve **Financial Freedom**. Rather than viewing finance as stressful budgeting, FreedomOS models wealth through first-principles mathematics, game theory, and high-leverage execution.

### The 5 Integrated Engines:

1. **The FIRE & Freedom Engine**:
   - Calculates your exact **Freedom Number** based on annual burn and Safe Withdrawal Rate (SWR, 3.5%–4.0%).
   - Simulates compound growth across Lean FIRE, Coast FIRE, Full FIRE, and Fat FIRE.
   - Includes an integrated **Monte Carlo Stress-Testing Engine** (300 economic scenarios accounting for sequence of returns risk).

2. **Cash Flow & High-Leverage Income Engine**:
   - Tracks active career compensation, side-hustles, freelancing, and automated passive income (dividends, SaaS, digital products).
   - Real-time **Savings Rate Velocity** indicator with the exponential retirement timeline ladder.

3. **The Debt Slayer (Snowball vs. Avalanche)**:
   - Eliminates high-interest consumer debt with dual algorithms:
     - **Avalanche** (sorts by highest APR first to mathematically save maximum interest).
     - **Snowball** (sorts by lowest balance first for rapid psychological momentum).
   - Payoff date simulator with extra monthly payment allocation.

4. **Asset Allocation & Net Worth Ledger**:
   - Diversification donut chart across Equities, Cash Buffers, Real Estate, Crypto, and Fixed Income.
   - Liquid Runway telemetry (months of living expenses if all active labor stopped today).

5. **The 7 Stages of Freedom & Daily Habit Tracker**:
   - Guides you across the 7 concrete levels of financial independence:
     1. Financial Clarity
     2. Debt Elimination
     3. Ironclad Runway (6 months emergency fund)
     4. Coast FIRE (compound growth takes over)
     5. Lean FIRE (basic survival needs covered)
     6. Full Financial Independence (work is 100% optional)
     7. Abundance & Fat FIRE (luxury & legacy wealth)
   - Daily high-ROI habits with streak counters and celebratory feedback.

## 🔒 100% Private & Local-First

- All your financial data is saved directly in your browser's `localStorage`.
- Zero analytics, zero trackers, zero cloud database leakage.
- Full one-click JSON backup and restore functionality.
